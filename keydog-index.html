Privacy Policy for KeyDog - File Encryption

Last updated: November 2026

KeyDog - File Encryption ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data handling and security when you use the KeyDog mobile application.

1. Core Principle: Local Processing Only

Local Encryption and Decryption: KeyDog operates strictly as an offline-first file security utility. All cryptographic operations (encryption, decryption, and key derivation) are performed entirely on your device's local hardware.

Zero Network File Transfer: We do not operate external file servers, remote databases, or cloud storage. Your selected files, encrypted containers, file names, and directories never leave your device.

No Password Recovery: Passwords, PINs, or encryption keys are processed ephemerally in memory or within device-level keystores. We do not transmit or store your passwords. If you lose your decryption key or password, we cannot recover your encrypted data.

2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All financial transactions, subscriptions, and entitlement verifications are handled securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

3. Permissions Used

  • Storage / Manage External Storage: Required solely to read the files you choose to encrypt or decrypt, and to write the resulting files back to your device storage.
  • Internet: Used exclusively for Google Play in-app billing verification and license status checks. It is never used to transmit or upload your files or user content.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used optionally to show progress notifications during ongoing batch file encryption or decryption tasks.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates Google Play Services strictly for billing and licensing management. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or data brokers. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy regarding how payment transaction details are handled.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
